  - Printed or digital checklist

risk_notes:
  - Never negotiate under the influence of alcohol or recreational substances.
  - Treat every "maybe" as a "no" unless explicit conditions are stated and agreed.
  - Both partners hold equal authority to pause or stop at any moment.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Container
      items:
        - id: container-datetime
          label: Date, time, and location confirmed
        - id: container-endtime
          label: Expected end time and hard-stop time agreed
        - id: container-presence
          label: Who is present (and who is not) confirmed
        - id: container-privacy
          label: Privacy, locks, phones, and interruption plan set
    - section: Activities In & Out
      items:
        - id: act-in
          label: Activities explicitly listed and agreed
        - id: act-curve
          label: Order and intensity curve discussed
        - id: act-hard-limits
          label: Hard limits named clearly by both partners
        - id: act-soft-limits
          label: Soft limits and exact conditions noted
    - section: Health & Body
      items:
        - id: health-injuries
          label: Injuries, joints, back, or breathing issues disclosed
        - id: health-meds
          label: Medications, allergies (latex, leather dyes, lube), and substances reviewed
        - id: health-sti
          label: STI status, PrEP/DoxyPEP context, and barrier expectations aligned
    - section: Marks, Power & Safewords
      items:
        - id: marks-visibility
          label: Marks acceptability, body zones, and fade duration agreed
        - id: power-language
          label: Titles, forms of address, praise/degradation preferences set
        - id: safewords-signals
          label: Red / Yellow / Green + non-verbal signal tested and confirmed
    - section: Aftercare Plan
      items:
        - id: aftercare-immediate
          label: Immediate water, snacks, warmth, and contact preferences set
        - id: aftercare-nextday
          label: Next-day check-in window and communication channel agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Opening the conversation
      line: "Before we start, I want to walk through a few things so we're both clear and on the same page."
    - context: Framing a soft limit
      line: "I have a soft limit on X. Here's what would make it a yes tonight, and here's what keeps it off the table."
    - context: Confirming marks
      line: "Are marks okay tonight? If yes, where are they off-limits and how long do they need to be faded by?"
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Sit together outside the play space with phones silenced.
    - Walk through each section without rushing or defending limits.
    - Summarize the agreed container, hard limits, and safewords out loud.
  headspace: Grounded, honest, unhurried. Clarity here is what unlocks intensity later.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Container & Logistics
    duration_min: 5
    description: Confirm location, privacy, phones off/on silent, and hard-stop time.
  - order: 2
    title: Activities, Limits & Health
    duration_min: 15
    description: Review what is in, what is out, any conditional maybes, and physical/health check-ins.
  - order: 3
    title: Power, Language & Safewords
    duration_min: 10
    description: Align on titles, tone, check-in cadence, and test both verbal and non-verbal stop signals.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Aftercare & Transition
    duration_min: 10
    description: Confirm post-scene needs and agree on the exact cue that begins the scene.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Quick 10-Minute Check-In
    summary: Streamlined version for established partners doing a familiar scene.
    adjustments:
      - Focus on Container, Health/Energy today, Marks, and Safeword confirmation.
  heavier:
    title: Multi-Day / High-Protocol Negotiation
    summary: Expanded deep-dive for weekend protocols, travel, or intense physical scenes.
    adjustments:
      - Add written notes for meal/sleep schedules, public vs private rules, and emergency exit logistics.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Confirm the scene start cue and take a breath before transitioning.
  physical:
    - Keep water and a towel staged near the play area before starting.
  emotional:
    - Reinforce that changing a "yes" to a "no" later is always respected.
  next_day:
    - Confirm exact time for next-day text or call.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did anything feel rushed or unclear during negotiation?
    - Were there any boundaries we want to adjust before next time?
  check_in_window: Immediately after negotiation and within 24 hours post-scene.
