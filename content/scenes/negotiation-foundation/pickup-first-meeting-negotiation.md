duration_min: 10
duration_max: 20
intensity: light
roles: ["Top / Host", "Bottom / Guest"]
experience_level: beginner
tier: free
materials:
  - Phone or printed pocket card
  - Water and basic safer-sex supplies
prep_time_min: 5
risk_notes:
  - First-time play carries higher uncertainty; keep intensity conservative and check-ins frequent.
  - Confirm an exact exit time and tell a trusted friend your general check-in window if meeting someone new.
  - Never skip STI/barrier alignment or safeword testing with a new partner.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Fast Container Check
      items:
        - id: pickup-time
          label: Hard-stop time and host/guest expectations clear
        - id: pickup-sober
          label: Substance/alcohol state disclosed honestly
        - id: pickup-health
          label: Injuries, allergies, and STI/PrEP/barrier rules aligned
    - section: Tonight's Scope
      items:
        - id: pickup-yes
          label: 2–3 core activities agreed for tonight
        - id: pickup-no
          label: Top 3–5 hard limits stated clearly
        - id: pickup-marks
          label: Marks policy (usually no visible marks on first meeting) confirmed
    - section: Safety & Exit
      items:
        - id: pickup-safeword
          label: Red / Yellow / Green + tap-out signal tested
        - id: pickup-aftercare
          label: Post-scene plan (water, hangout vs. heading home) agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Setting the tone at the door or bar
      line: "Before we get into it, let's take five minutes to line up hard limits, safewords, and what kind of pace feels good tonight."
    - context: Asking about marks & STI
      line: "I'm on PrEP and prefer no visible marks above the collar—how does that line up for you?"
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Have this conversation with clothes on, over water, before any gear or restraint comes out.
    - Keep the first scene shorter and lighter than your theoretical max.
  headspace: Alert, respectful, clear-eyed.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Arrival & Vibe Check
    duration_min: 5
    description: Offer water, confirm comfort, and verify both people feel ready to proceed.
  - order: 2
    title: 5-Minute Core Limits & Health
    duration_min: 5
    description: Cover hard limits, injuries, safer-sex expectations, and visible marks.
  - order: 3
    title: Safeword & Exit Plan
    duration_min: 5
    description: Test verbal and non-verbal stop signals and confirm post-scene transition.
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Bar / Event Backroom Check
    summary: Ultra-compact 3-minute verbal check for semi-public event spaces.
    adjustments:
      - Confirm hard limits, tap-out signal, barriers, and visibility before moving to play area.
  heavier:
    title: Pre-Booked Hotel / Dungeon Session
    summary: Full 20-minute structured intake for a planned first-time intensive scene.
    adjustments:
      - Exchange written checklist via share link 24 hours in advance.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - De-gear together, offer water, and confirm safe ride/transit home.
  physical:
    - Check any skin or wrists for unexpected redness.
  emotional:
    - Keep expectations realistic—first scenes are about calibration, not perfection.
  next_day:
    - Send a brief next-day message to confirm both feel good.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the pace feel right for a first time together?
    - Is there anything we'd add or drop if we play again?
  check_in_window: Next morning.
