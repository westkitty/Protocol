duration_min: 15
duration_max: 30
intensity: medium
roles: ["Partner A", "Partner B"]
experience_level: intermediate
tier: pro
materials:
  - Printed script guide
  - Timer for gradual test intervals
prep_time_min: 5
risk_notes:
  - Never pressure a partner to turn a hard limit into a soft limit.
  - Build an explicit "graceful abort" into any test run so stopping feels like a success, not a failure.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Defining the Edge
      items:
        - id: soft-identify
          label: Specific activity named (not vague categories)
        - id: soft-why
          label: Underlying concern identified (pain, claustrophobia, embarrassment, physical safety)
        - id: soft-conditions
          label: Exact conditions required to test (warm-up, duration cap, position)
    - section: Guardrails
      items:
        - id: soft-cap
          label: Maximum test duration set (e.g., 2 minutes)
        - id: soft-yellow
          label: Yellow = pause and adjust; Red = immediate stop without debate
        - id: soft-no-shame
          label: Explicit agreement that aborting carries zero disappointment
  scripts:
    - context: Naming a soft limit
      line: "I'm curious about X, but it's a soft limit for me because of Y. If we try it for two minutes after a full warm-up, I'm willing to test it."
    - context: Top reassuring the Bottom
      line: "If you call Yellow or Red five seconds in, that's a win because we learned where the line is. Zero pressure to push through."
    - context: Checking in mid-test
      line: "Give me a color and a number from 1 to 10 on how intense that feels right now."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Agree that soft-limit testing happens early or mid-scene while energy and judgment are clear.
    - Test the abort signal physically before beginning.
  headspace: Experimental, patient, safety-first.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Root Concern & Conditions
    duration_min: 10
    description: Identify what makes the activity a "maybe" and write down the exact conditions.
  - order: 2
    title: Micro-Test Design
    duration_min: 10
    description: Set a short timer (1–3 minutes) and define a clear exit ramp into familiar play.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Immediate Calibration
    duration_min: 5
    description: Decide how you will debrief right after the test before moving on.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Conceptual Talk-Through Only
    summary: Discuss the fantasy or mechanics verbally without physical testing tonight.
    adjustments:
      - Keep clothes on and treat it purely as future planning.
  heavier:
    title: Tiered Progressive Test
    summary: Three short rounds (30s, 60s, 120s) with mandatory green-light check-ins between each.
    adjustments:
      - Stop immediately if any round drops below an enthusiastic yes.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Pivot to a comforting or familiar beat right after the test.
  physical:
    - Offer water, warmth, or grounding touch.
  emotional:
    - Praise the communication regardless of whether the test continued or stopped.
  next_day:
    - Reclassify the activity in your Yes/No/Maybe list based on how it felt 24 hours later.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the guardrails make it feel safe to explore?
    - Does this move to Yes, stay Maybe with conditions, or move to Hard No?
  check_in_window: Immediately post-test and next day.
tags:

---

Soft limits are where growth happens safely. These scripts take the guesswork and performance pressure out of testing something new.
