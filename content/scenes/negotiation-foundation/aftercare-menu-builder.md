---
id: aftercare-menu-builder
title: Aftercare Menu Builder
discreet_title: Post-Session Recovery Planner
category: Negotiation & Foundation
category_slug: negotiation-foundation
subcategory: Aftercare
duration_min: 10
duration_max: 20
intensity: light
roles: ["Partner A", "Partner B"]
experience_level: beginner
tier: free
materials:
  - Water, electrolytes, salty/sweet snacks
  - Warm blanket or hoodie
  - Towels and gentle skin care
prep_time_min: 5
risk_notes:
  - Both tops and bottoms can experience drop (sub drop or top drop) 12–48 hours later.
  - Never leave a partner alone immediately after an intense scene without verifying they are grounded.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Physical Care
      items:
        - id: aft-hydration
          label: Water, electrolytes, or warm tea staged
        - id: aft-food
          label: Quick blood-sugar snack (chocolate, fruit, crackers) ready
        - id: aft-temp
          label: Blanket, hoodie, or cool cloth for temperature swings
        - id: aft-cleanup
          label: Shower together, solo wash-up, or warm towel wipe-down
    - section: Contact & Space
      items:
        - id: aft-touch
          label: Heavy cuddling / held tightly vs. light touch vs. no-touch space
        - id: aft-gear
          label: Gear/collar removed immediately vs. kept on during wind-down
    - section: Emotional & Communication
      items:
        - id: aft-talk
          label: Quiet silence vs. light chat vs. immediate verbal praise/debrief
        - id: aft-nextday
          label: Next-day text/call time and drop-support plan agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Asking before the scene
      line: "When we wrap up tonight, do you usually want to be held under a blanket, or do you need ten minutes of quiet space and water first?"
    - context: Checking in during aftercare
      line: "You did great. Take your time—do you want more water, a blanket, or just to lie here quietly?"
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Stage water, snacks, and blankets before the scene starts so you don't scramble afterward.
  headspace: Attentive, unhurried, protective.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Physical Reset
    duration_min: 5
    description: Offer water, remove tight restraints/gear, and regulate body temperature.
  - order: 2
    title: Contact & Grounding
    duration_min: 10
    description: Follow the pre-selected contact preference (held, parallel rest, or quiet space).
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Casual Wind-Down
    summary: Simple water, towel off, and 10 minutes of relaxed conversation.
    adjustments:
      - Appropriate for light scenes or brief check-ins.
  heavier:
    title: Deep Drop Recovery Protocol
    summary: Full 45-minute sensory grounding, electrolytes, sugar, warmth, and next-day check-in schedule.
    adjustments:
      - Check in at 12 hours, 24 hours, and 48 hours post-scene.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Confirm both partners feel steady before anyone drives or leaves.
  physical:
    - Replenish fluids and sodium/sugar.
  emotional:
    - Reaffirm appreciation and care out of role.
  next_day:
    - Follow through on the scheduled check-in even if the scene felt "easy."
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the aftercare match what your body and head actually needed?
  check_in_window: Next day.
tags:
  - aftercare
  - drop
  - recovery
  - starter
---

Build a custom aftercare plan before the scene begins—when both brains are sharp—so neither partner has to guess what the other needs afterward.
