  - Bruising and rope marks can take 24 hours to peak and 5–14 days to fade.

negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Visibility Zones
      items:
        - id: mrk-face-neck
          label: Face, neck, and behind-the-ears (usually hard no for work visibility)
        - id: mrk-arms-legs
          label: Forearms, wrists, calves (short-sleeve / gym shorts visibility)
        - id: mrk-torso
          label: Chest, back, thighs, and ass (covered by standard clothing)
    - section: Duration & Type
      items:
        - id: mrk-duration
          label: Maximum acceptable fade time (same-night redness vs. multi-day bruise)
        - id: mrk-upcoming
          label: Upcoming work travel, family events, doctor visits, or beach trips checked
        - id: mrk-tools
          label: Tools likely to mark (cane, strap, crop, clamps, teeth) explicitly approved
  scripts:
    - context: Setting clear coverage rules
      line: "Above the t-shirt line and below the shorts line need to stay completely clear—anywhere covered by briefs or a t-shirt is fair game."
    - context: Checking intensity mid-scene
      line: "We're getting some solid redness on your thighs; do you want to keep building here or keep it under bruising?"
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Physically trace the clothing coverage line (t-shirt collar, sleeves, shorts hem) together.
    - Note if the bottom bruises easily or takes blood thinners/NSAIDs.
  headspace: Intentional, mindful of real-world life outside the scene.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Clothing Line Mapping
    duration_min: 5
    description: Define exact no-mark zones vs. open zones on the body.
  - order: 2
    title: Intensity & Tool Alignment
    duration_min: 5
    description: Match tool selection (broad paddles vs. stingy crops/canes) to mark tolerance.
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Zero-Mark / Warmth Only
    summary: Broad-surface impact and thuddy tools only; stop before petechiae or bruising.
    adjustments:
      - Use open hand, wide leather paddle, or soft deerskin flogger.
  heavier:
    title: Intentional Badge / Collar Marks
    summary: Deliberate multi-day marks in covered zones as a reminder of ownership or intensity.
    adjustments:
      - Confirm upcoming 7–10 day calendar is clear of medical or family exposure.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Apply cool compress or arnica gel if desired to reduce swelling.
  physical:
    - Moisturize skin and avoid hot showers immediately if skin is abraded.
  emotional:
    - Check how the bottom feels seeing the marks in the mirror afterward.
  next_day:
    - Send a photo check-in if needed to track how marks settled.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the marks stay within agreed zones and intensity?
  check_in_window: Next morning (when bruising typically peaks).
