  - Prolonged denial can trigger irritability, anxiety, or sleeplessness in some men; daily check-ins catch this early so you can adjust.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Daily Metrics
      items:
        - id: dci-physical
          label: Physical comfort check (1–5) + any ache/chafing reported honestly
        - id: dci-mood
          label: Mood & sleep quality reported honestly
        - id: dci-arousal
          label: Arousal level (1–10) and obedience/focus check
  scripts:
    - context: Daily evening check-in message
      line: "Day 3 Check-In: Physical 5/5 (zero pinch), Sleep 4/5, Arousal 8/10. Focus is sharp, staying hands-off."
    - context: Keyholder response
      line: "Good boy. Keep your hands off tonight—if sleep gets restless, take a cool shower and text me in the morning."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Agree on the exact daily check-in time (e.g., 9:00 PM) and whether it's in-person or text.
  headspace: Honest, concise, connected.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: 3-Metric Report
    duration_min: 5
    description: Denied partner reports Physical Comfort, Mood/Sleep, and Arousal cleanly.
    check_in: true
  - order: 2
    title: Directive or Adjustment
    duration_min: 5
    description: Keyholder gives praise, a teasing task, or a practical adjustment (e.g., unlock for sleep).
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Quick Green/Yellow/Red Ping
    summary: One-word color status plus arousal 1–10.
    adjustments:
      - Takes under 30 seconds by text.
  heavier:
    title: Formal Evening Kneeling Report + Inspection
    summary: In-person 10-minute report with visual device/skin check and optional teasing.
    adjustments:
      - Always keep physical comfort reporting out-of-character and honest.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Acknowledge every check-in promptly so the denied partner never feels ignored.
  physical:
    - Address any physical discomfort immediately.
  emotional:
    - Balance teasing with genuine care.
  next_day:
    - Repeat at scheduled time.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the daily check-in cadence keep tension high without feeling burdensome?
  check_in_window: Daily at agreed hour.
