# Multi-Day Denial Track

A multi-day track works when the rules are simple enough to remember and the check-ins are consistent. This structure gives a clear daily rhythm, sample language for each check-in, and the ways those messages or conversations are invoked.

## Sample 5-Day Structure

**Day 0 (Activation)**  
In-person or clear message: rules restated, start time confirmed, first check-in scheduled.

**Days 1–4 (Daily)**  
- Morning: short status (energy, physical comfort, mindset).
- Evening: longer check-in (what was difficult, any permission requests, confirmation of rules).

**Mid-point (Day 3)**  
Longer conversation or call to adjust if needed.

**Final Day**  
Explicit decision: continue, modify, or end with a defined release.

## Dialogue Options & Invocation Methods

### Daily Morning Check-In
**Options:**
- "Morning status: energy [1–10], comfort [1–10], anything needed?"
- "Still following the rules. No issues."
- Controlling partner sends: "Check in."

**How to invoke:**
- Agreed time window (e.g., before 10 a.m.).
- A single emoji or one-word prompt from the controlling partner that requires a reply.
- Automatic reminder both people set.

### Evening Check-In
**Options:**
- "Evening report: what was hard today, any requests, confirmation I'm still in."
- "I need to pause the track."
- "Permission to [specific act]?"

**How to invoke:**
- Required reply to a standing evening message.
- Voice note instead of text if that was agreed.
- In-person version: kneel or sit and give the report out loud.

### Permission Requests
**Options:**
- "May I edge?"
- "Requesting touch for ten minutes."
- "No request tonight."

**How to invoke:**
- Must use the exact request format.
- Controlling partner answers only with "Yes," "No," "Later," or a counter-offer.
- Silence for more than the agreed time counts as "not now."

### Ending or Pausing
**Options:**
- "I am pausing the track."
- "Track ends tonight at [time]."
- "We continue under the same rules."

**How to invoke:**
- Either person can send the pause phrase; it is effective immediately.
- End is confirmed by both people restating the return to normal permissions.

## Why This Works
Daily low-friction check-ins surface problems before they become resentment or dropped limits. The language stays short so it remains usable even when arousal or frustration is high.

## Adjustable Elements
- Length (3, 5, 7 days).
- Device or no device.
- How many permission requests are allowed per day.
- Whether orgasm is fully denied or earned on the final day.
