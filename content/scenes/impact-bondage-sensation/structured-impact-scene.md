  checklist:

      items:
        - id: sis-zones

        - id: sis-tools
          label: Specific tools approved (hand, flogger, paddle, strap, crop)
        - id: sis-marks

pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Inspect all floggers/paddles for rough edges, loose rivets, or split leather.
    - Warm up the room slightly so muscles aren't tense and cold.
  headspace: Rhythmic, present, endorphin-driven.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Touch & Warm-Up (Hand / Light Flogger)
    duration_min: 10
    description: Broad, rhythmic contact to warm blood flow, relax breathing, and establish rhythm.
  - order: 2
    title: Progressive Escalation
    duration_min: 15
    description: Increase speed/weight or transition to firmer paddle/strap with a mandatory mid-beat color check.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Peak Intensity
    duration_min: 10
    description: Focused, intense peak within agreed limits while watching breath and body tension.
  - order: 4
    title: Cool-Down & Grounding Touch
    duration_min: 10
    description: Step back down to light hand strokes, rubbing warmth into skin, and deep breaths.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Warm-Up & Endorphin Float
    summary: Stay at warm hand spanking and soft suede flogger for a relaxing, zero-bruise float.
    adjustments:
      - Focus on steady rhythm and breath synchronization.
  heavier:
    title: Heavy Thud & Sting Contrast
    summary: Alternate heavy leather paddle thud with sharp crop/strap sting after a 15-minute warm-up.
    adjustments:
      - Keep check-ins explicit between tool swaps.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Offer water immediately; endorphin drop can cause chills or dizziness.
  physical:
    - Cover with a warm blanket and apply cooling gel/arnica if requested.
  emotional:
    - Hold or sit close while breathing returns to baseline.
  next_day:
    - Check how skin and soreness feel 24 hours later.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - How did the warm-up-to-peak curve feel?
    - Which tool felt best tonight?
  check_in_window: Right after cool-down and next morning.

Great impact play is built on rhythm and temperature, not just force. A proper warm-up and cool-down unlock deeper endorphins and protect tissue.
