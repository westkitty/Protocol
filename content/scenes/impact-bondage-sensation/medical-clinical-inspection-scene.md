roles: ["Examiner / Sir", "Subject / Patient"]

materials:
  - Nitrile gloves (check latex allergies!)
  - Bright directional light or exam lamp
  - Clean sheet/table, stethoscope or cold metal spoon/Wartenberg wheel (single-user or sanitized)
  - Measuring tape, lube (if internal exam negotiated)
prep_time_min: 15
risk_notes:
  - Never use sharp or skin-breaking tools (Wartenberg wheels, needles) without explicit negotiation and single-person/autoclave sterilization protocols.
  - Strictly verify latex vs. nitrile glove allergies beforehand.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Exam Scope & Hygiene
      items:
        - id: mci-allergies
          label: Glove material confirmed (nitrile default) and metal/temperature sensitivity checked
        - id: mci-depth
          label: Exam scope agreed (external visual/touch, posture/flexibility, or internal gloved exam)
        - id: mci-tools
          label: Exact tools approved (light, stethoscope, cold metal, pinwheel, speculum)
    - section: Tone & Boundaries
      items:
        - id: mci-tone
          label: Clinical detachment vs. stern authority tone agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Starting the exam
      line: "Stand on the sheet, feet shoulder-width apart. Answer only what I ask, and hold still while I check."
    - context: Maintaining safety
      line: "Tell me if the cold metal or pressure crosses from sharp into Yellow."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Sanitize all non-porous tools with 70% isopropyl alcohol and let dry completely.
    - Stage nitrile gloves, clean towels, and exam light.
  headspace: Detached, clinical, hyper-attentive to small physical details.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Intake & Posture Measurements
    duration_min: 10
    description: Examiner puts on gloves, inspects posture, skin, and breathing calmly.
  - order: 2
    title: Sensory & Reflex Assessment
    duration_min: 15
    description: Test cold/warm contrast, light touch vs. firm pressure, and heartbeat/breath checks.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Negotiated Focused Exam & Closure
    duration_min: 15
    description: Focused genital/body exam as negotiated, followed by clean release and towel-off.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Non-Invasive Physical Readiness Check
    summary: Gloves, flashlight, stethoscope, and posture/muscle inspection only—no sharp tools or internal play.
    adjustments:
      - Great for mixing into leather or pup intake scenes.
  heavier:
    title: Restraint Table + Cold/Sharp Sensation Exam
    summary: Subject strapped to bench/bed with blindfold while examiner tests temperature, pinwheel, and prolonged stillness.
    adjustments:
      - Use single-user pinwheel only on intact skin; never draw blood without dedicated edge-play protocols.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Remove gloves, warm up the subject with a blanket, and shift out of clinical detachment.
  physical:
    - Wipe off lube/gel and offer warm water or tea.
  emotional:
    - Reconnect warmly—clinical detachment can feel stark, so intentional warmth afterward matters.
  next_day:
    - Brief check-in.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the clinical tone feel exciting and safe?
  check_in_window: Right after warm-up/blanket reset.

Clinical detachment—gloves snap, bright light, measured voice—creates intense vulnerability. Warm, connected aftercare afterward completes the contrast.
