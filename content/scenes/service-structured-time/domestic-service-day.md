  - Cleaning supplies & water

  - Never use bleach or strong chemical cleaners in enclosed spaces without ventilation and gloves.
  - Keep tasks realistic for the time window; domestic service is about pride and precision, not impossible busywork.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Task & Attire Scope
      items:
        - id: dsd-tasks
          label: Exact list of 4–6 household tasks agreed (no surprise deep-clean hazards)
        - id: dsd-attire
          label: Service attire agreed (boots + jock, shorts, apron, or collar)
        - id: dsd-inspection
          label: Inspection criteria and praise/reward at completion set
  scripts:
    - context: Starting the day
      line: "Here's your list for the morning. Work steadily, keep water handy, and report to me when the kitchen is reset."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Verify all cleaning supplies, gloves, and trash bags are stocked.
  headspace: Industrious, proud, detail-oriented.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Briefing & Attire Check
    duration_min: 15
    description: Review the task list, dress in agreed service attire, and start timer.
  - order: 2
    title: Morning Task Block
    duration_min: 120
    description: Execute kitchen, laundry, or living-space tasks while Sir works or relaxes nearby.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Inspection & Reward
    duration_min: 30
    description: Sir inspects each completed room, gives specific praise, and transitions to rest or play.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 90-Minute Sunday Reset
    summary: Three quick tasks (bedding, dishes, trash) followed by coffee together.
    adjustments:
      - Play music and keep conversation light.
  heavier:
    title: Silent White-Glove Household Reset
    summary: 5-hour full apartment reset in jock and collar with silent inspection at the end of each hour.
    adjustments:
      - Include a 20-minute lunch break.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Shower off cleaning dust and change into comfortable clothes.
  physical:
    - Stretch lower back and shoulders.
  emotional:
    - Genuine appreciation for visible effort.
  next_day:
    - None required unless paired with heavier play.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the task list feel satisfying and fair?
  check_in_window: Right after final inspection.
