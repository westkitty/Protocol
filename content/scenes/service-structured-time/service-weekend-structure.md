  - Sleep, hydration, and actual meals are non-negotiable across a 48-hour container.

  checklist:
    - section: Weekend Container
      items:
        - id: sw-arrival
          label: Friday evening arrival, negotiation refresh, and collar/protocol activation time set
        - id: sw-blocks
          label: Saturday morning, afternoon, and evening service blocks + defined free time mapped
        - id: sw-exit
          label: Sunday de-escalation, explicit end time, and aftercare window locked
    - section: Service Types & Check-Ins
      items:
        - id: sw-tasks
          label: Domestic tasks, leather care, protocol observance, and optional sexual service agreed
        - id: sw-checkins
          label: Morning check-in, midday adjustment window, and evening review scheduled
  scripts:
    - context: Friday evening activation
      line: "Collar goes on at 8 PM. Saturday has three service blocks with a two-hour afternoon reset, and we close cleanly Sunday at 3 PM."
    - context: Midday adjustment window
      line: "We're in our midday check-in—how's your energy, knees, and headspace? Anything need scaling for tonight?"
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Grocery shop and stage cleaning/leather supplies before Friday so service flows smoothly.
    - Post the block schedule on the fridge or share it digitally.
  headspace: Purposeful, rhythmic, sustainable endurance.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Friday Arrival & Protocol Activation
    duration_min: 90
    description: Arrival, negotiation refresh, collar/protocol activation, and settling in.
  - order: 2
    title: Saturday Morning Service & Check-In
    duration_min: 240
    description: Morning coffee/breakfast service, domestic reset, and 15-minute morning check-in.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Saturday Afternoon Leather & Free Time
    duration_min: 240
    description: Leather/boot care block followed by scheduled unscripted rest/free time.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Saturday Evening Dinner & Main Scene
    duration_min: 240
    description: Evening meal service, formal review, and negotiated evening play/sexual service.
    check_in: true
  - order: 5
    title: Sunday De-Escalation & Debrief
    duration_min: 180
    description: Gradual return to baseline, explicit collar removal, aftercare window, and debrief.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 24-Hour Saturday Service Intensive
    summary: Run Saturday 9 AM to Sunday 9 AM with two focused service blocks and relaxed evening.
    adjustments:
      - Omit formal dinner protocol; keep casual meals together.
  heavier:
    title: Zero-Free-Time Strict Weekend
    summary: Every hour from Friday 7 PM to Sunday 4 PM is assigned (including structured rest).
    adjustments:
      - Keep scheduled 15-minute out-of-role check-ins morning, midday, and night.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Explicit end-time announcement and collar removal.
  physical:
    - Rest, hydrate, and share a relaxed Sunday meal without service roles.
  emotional:
    - Acknowledge the labor and care put into the weekend.
  next_day:
    - Next-day message required to check for post-weekend drop.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Which service block felt most fulfilling?
    - Did the midday adjustment window catch any fatigue early?
  check_in_window: Sunday afternoon and Monday message.
