discreet_title: Equipment Staging & Offering Ritual
category: Leather & Gear
category_slug: leather-gear
subcategory: Rituals
duration_min: 20
duration_max: 40
intensity: light
roles: ["Top / Sir", "Bottom / Boy"]
experience_level: beginner
tier: pro
materials:
  - Clean tray, leather mat, or dark towel
  - Selected toys/gear for the upcoming scene (flogger, cuffs, lube, collar)
prep_time_min: 10
risk_notes:
  - Presenting a toy on the tray is an opportunity to confirm it is still a "yes" tonight—never pressure an item that was marked "no" or "maybe."
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Tray Contents
      items:
        - id: gpr-items
          label: Every item placed on the tray has already been negotiated as "yes"
        - id: gpr-posture
          label: Presentation posture (kneeling, standing, eyes down/up) agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Presenting the tray
      line: "Set the tray at my feet, kneel behind it, and tell me which piece you want most tonight."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Wipe down all toys and check cuffs/stitching before staging them on the tray.
  headspace: Ceremonial, anticipatory, intentional.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Staging & Approach
    duration_min: 5
    description: Bottom brings the arranged tray into the play space and presents it at Top's feet.
  - order: 2
    title: Selection & Confirmation
    duration_min: 10
    description: Top picks up 2–3 items, tests them lightly in hand, and confirms verbal readiness.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Scene Launch
    duration_min: 5
    description: Tray is set aside safely and the main scene begins.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Choice of Two
    summary: Top lays two toys on the bed and asks the bottom to hand over the one they want first.
    adjustments:
      - Simple, conversational, zero kneeling required.
  heavier:
    title: Silent Altar Offering
    summary: Bottom kneels motionless holding the tray for 3 minutes while Top circles and selects.
    adjustments:
      - Keep weight light so forearms don't cramp.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Clean and put away all used gear together or as closing service.
  physical:
    - Water and rest.
  emotional:
    - Acknowledge the anticipation and trust built before a single stroke landed.
  next_day:
    - None required beyond main scene debrief.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did presenting the gear build excitement and clarity?
  check_in_window: End of main scene.
tags:
  - gear
  - presentation
  - ritual
  - anticipation
---

A clean, focused pre-scene ritual that turns toy staging into a moment of formal offering and consent confirmation.
