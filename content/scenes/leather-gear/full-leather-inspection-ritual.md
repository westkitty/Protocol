title: Full Leather Inspection Ritual
discreet_title: Comprehensive Gear & Posture Review
category: Leather & Gear
category_slug: leather-gear
subcategory: Inspection
duration_min: 30
duration_max: 60
intensity: medium
roles: ["Sir / Leather Top", "Boy / Leather Sub"]
experience_level: intermediate
tier: pro
materials:
  - Leather boots, chaps/breeches, harness, belt, vest, or cap
  - Microfiber cloth & pocket flashlight (optional)
  - Inspection mat or bare floor mark
prep_time_min: 15
risk_notes:
  - Heavy leather can cause overheating during long standing inspections; watch for lightheadedness or locked knees.
  - Instruct the bottom to keep knees slightly unlocked while standing at attention.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Inspection Scope
      items:
        - id: fli-pieces
          label: Full gear list to be worn and inspected confirmed
        - id: fli-touch
          label: Touch boundaries during inspection (buckle adjustments, pat-down, strap tension) agreed
        - id: fli-correction
          label: Response to scuffs, crooked buckles, or loose laces agreed
    - section: Physical Comfort
      items:
        - id: fli-knees
          label: Standing duration limit (max 15–20 min before movement) set
        - id: fli-safeword
          label: Verbal safeword and slight sway/tap cue confirmed
  scripts:
    - context: Calling to attention
      line: "Heels together, shoulders back, hands behind you. Don't lock your knees. Let's see how you look."
    - context: Inspecting leather
      line: "Boots have a mirror toe, belt tongue is centered. One smudge on the left buckle—note that for after."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Give the bottom 20 minutes to dress and polish before inspection begins.
    - Keep water nearby in case heavy leather warms the room.
  headspace: Crisp, proud, classic leather discipline.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Attention & Visual Walkaround
    duration_min: 10
    description: Sir circles slowly from boots to cap, assessing posture, symmetry, and polish.
  - order: 2
    title: Hardware & Fit Check
    duration_min: 15
    description: Sir tests strap tension, harness alignment, and boot lacing with deliberate hands.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Pass / Correction & Release
    duration_min: 10
    description: Formal pass ("You wear that leather well") or minor polish task, then transition to play or bar.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Harness & Boots Check
    summary: 15-minute pre-bar inspection wearing just boots, jeans/chaps, and chest harness.
    adjustments:
      - Keep tone warm and encouraging, focused on getting ready together.
  heavier:
    title: White-Glove Demerit Inspection
    summary: Strict 45-minute inspection where every dust speck or crooked keeper earns a pre-negotiated swat.
    adjustments:
      - Schedule a seated water break halfway through.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Unbuckle tight vests/harnesses if staying home, or head out to the event sharp.
  physical:
    - Stretch calves and lower back after standing at attention.
  emotional:
    - Genuine pride and affirmation in how the leather looks.
  next_day:
    - Wipe down sweat from inside harness/boots before storing.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the inspection feel sharp and affirming?
  check_in_window: Immediately after inspection or post-event.
tags:
  - leather
  - inspection
  - posture
  - ritual
---

Nothing builds leather pride like a deliberate, unhurried inspection—honoring the gear, the posture, and the work that went into both.
