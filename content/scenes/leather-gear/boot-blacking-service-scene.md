  - Leather boots

  - Saddle soap, conditioner, cream/wax polish, water cup

prep_time_min: 10
risk_notes:
  - Wax polishes and solvents have fumes; work in a ventilated room.
  - Kneeling over boots strains knees and lower back—use a thick mat or low stool.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Bootblacking Rules
      items:
        - id: bb-boots-on
          label: Boots worn on feet vs. placed on boot stand agreed
        - id: bb-tongue
          label: Strictly cloth/brush polish vs. mouth/spit-shine boundaries confirmed (chemical safety!)
        - id: bb-talking
          label: Quiet focus vs. casual conversation vs. formal service address agreed
    - section: Physical Comfort
      items:
        - id: bb-posture
          label: Mat/stool provided and posture shift allowed if knees ache
  scripts:
    - context: Presenting boots
      line: "Sit on your mat. Take your time with the welt and laces first—I want to feel the brush rhythm."
    - context: Pausing for posture
      line: "Shift to the stool for the buffing pass so your knees stay fresh."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Lay down a dark towel or drop cloth to protect floors from black polish.
    - Stage brushes and polish in order of use (clean -> condition -> polish -> buff).
  headspace: Tactile, rhythmic, deeply grounded in leather heritage.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Tool Staging & Lace Check
    duration_min: 10
    description: Bootblack lays out tools neatly and unlaces or clears dust from the boots.
  - order: 2
    title: Clean & Condition
    duration_min: 15
    description: Damp wipe or saddle soap pass, followed by slow hand or cloth conditioning.
  - order: 3
    title: Polish & Mirror Buff
    duration_min: 20
    description: Wax/cream application in small circles, then brisk horsehair brush and cloth buffing.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Final Inspection & Praise
    duration_min: 10
    description: Owner inspects the shine in good light and rewards the bootblack.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Quick 20-Minute Pre-Bar Shine
    summary: Fast brush, cream polish, and buff on a low chair before heading out.
    adjustments:
      - Skip deep saddle-soap stripping; focus on toe cap and heel shine.
  heavier:
    title: Full Multi-Pair Service Block
    summary: Strip, condition, and mirror-shine 2–3 pairs of boots over 90 minutes in strict service silence.
    adjustments:
      - Mandatory 5-minute stretch and water break between pairs.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Wash polish off hands/forearms thoroughly with soap and warm water.
  physical:
    - Stretch back, shoulders, and knees.
  emotional:
    - Admire the finished boots together; affirm the skill and patience.
  next_day:
    - Store brushes cleanly so wax doesn't harden.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the pace feel meditative and hot?
    - Any wrist or knee fatigue we should adjust next time?
  check_in_window: Right after hand-washing.
