discreet_title: Group & Third-Party Session Alignment
category: Supporting Modules
category_slug: supporting-modules
subcategory: Multi-Partner
duration_min: 20
duration_max: 40
intensity: medium
roles: ["Host / Primary", "Partner", "Guest(s)"]
experience_level: intermediate
tier: pro
materials:
  - Shared checklist for all participants
  - Extra barriers, towels, water, and color-coded wristbands (optional)
prep_time_min: 15
risk_notes:
  - Every person in a group scene must independently consent—one person's "yes" never grants consent on behalf of another.
  - Designate one "safety anchor" or universal "RED!" rule where any single person calling Red pauses the entire room immediately.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Group Boundaries & Matrix
      items:
        - id: mpa-who-who
          label: Who can touch/play with whom explicitly mapped (not everyone has to play with everyone)
        - id: mpa-sti
          label: Barrier, glove, and fluid rules standardized across all participants
        - id: mpa-photos
          label: Strict no-phones / no-photos rule confirmed
    - section: Universal Safety
      items:
        - id: mpa-red
          label: Universal "Red" pauses the whole scene immediately until checked
        - id: mpa-exit
          label: Quiet cool-down room or exit option available for anyone needing a break
  scripts:
    - context: Group alignment circle
      line: "Before we start: anyone calling Red pauses the whole room. Let's go around once and name our hard limits and what we're excited for."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Hold a 10-minute plain-clothes circle with everyone present before play starts.
    - Stage separate fresh barriers/lube stations if multiple pairs are playing.
  headspace: Clear, respectful, unified on safety.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Circle Alignment & Limits
    duration_min: 15
    description: Each participant states their name, pronouns/title, hard limits, and yeses.
  - order: 2
    title: Pairing & Barrier Check
    duration_min: 10
    description: Confirm who is playing with whom and verify universal Red/Yellow rules.
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Three-Way / Guest Addition
    summary: Streamlined alignment when an established couple invites one guest.
    adjustments:
      - Clarify whether couple stays together or plays independently.
  heavier:
    title: Structured Play Party Container
    summary: 4–8 guys with a dedicated sober safety monitor (DM) and scheduled water breaks.
    adjustments:
      - Keep alcohol minimal and stage a quiet decompression area.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Ensure every guest has water, clean-up supplies, and a safe ride before leaving.
  physical:
    - Clean shared surfaces and stage fresh towels.
  emotional:
    - Check in with primary partner privately after guests depart, plus group text next day if desired.
  next_day:
    - Next-day check-in with both primary partner and guest(s).
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did everyone feel their boundaries were honored in the group energy?
  check_in_window: After guests depart and next day.
tags:
  - multi-partner

---

Add this module on top of any scene whenever a third partner or group is involved—because group chemistry only works when everyone's limits are crystal clear.
