  - Keep immediate debriefs brief and appreciative; save heavy critique or major rule overhauls for 24 hours later when adrenaline has settled.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: 3-Question Debrief
      items:
        - id: idt-highlight
          label: "1. What was your favorite moment or beat tonight?"
        - id: idt-tweak
          label: "2. Anything physically uncomfortable or worth tweaking next time?"
        - id: idt-headspace
          label: "3. On a 1–5 scale, how grounded do you feel right now?"
  scripts:
    - context: Opening a quick debrief
      line: "Before we crash, give me one highlight, one tiny tweak for next time, and how grounded you feel right now."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Wait until both partners have had water and at least 10 minutes of physical aftercare before asking debrief questions.
  headspace: Appreciative, relaxed, non-defensive.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Highlights & Wins
    duration_min: 5
    description: Each partner names one standout moment that worked really well.
  - order: 2
    title: Practical Tweaks & Grounding Check
    duration_min: 5
    description: Note any gear/posture adjustments and confirm both feel steady.
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 2-Minute Pillow Talk Check
    summary: Just "favorite moment + how are you feeling?"
    adjustments:
      - Zero note-taking; keep it effortless.
  heavier:
    title: Logged Personal Library Entry
    summary: Record ratings, mark checks, and gear notes directly into your Personal Library.
    adjustments:
      - Great for tracking progressive training tracks.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - End with affection or relaxed rest.
  physical:
    - Continue hydrating.
  emotional:
    - Celebrate what worked.
  next_day:
    - Follow up with Next-Day Check-In Template.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Do both partners feel complete and connected?
  check_in_window: 10–20 minutes post-scene.

A 5-minute structured debrief locks in what was hot, catches small gear tweaks while fresh, and confirms both partners are grounded.
