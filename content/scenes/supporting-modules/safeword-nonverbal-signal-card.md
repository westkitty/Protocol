risk_notes:
  - If a gag, pup hood, mitts, or non-verbal protocol prevents speech, a physical non-verbal signal is mandatory—never rely solely on spoken words.
  - Both Top and Bottom can call Yellow or Red at any time.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Verbal Signals
      items:
        - id: swc-green
          label: GREEN = All good, keep going / intensity is great
        - id: swc-yellow
          label: YELLOW = Slow down, check in, or adjust position/intensity without ending the scene
        - id: swc-red
          label: RED = Full stop immediately, no debate, transition to care
    - section: Non-Verbal Signals
      items:
        - id: swc-tap
          label: 3 rapid taps (on thigh, floor, or Top's arm) = RED / immediate stop
        - id: swc-drop
          label: Dropping a held item (ball, keys, bell) = RED / immediate stop
        - id: swc-squeeze
          label: 2 slow squeezes = YELLOW / pause & check in
  scripts:
    - context: Testing signals before starting
      line: "Say Red and Yellow out loud once so it's easy. And if you're hooded or gagged, three rapid taps on my leg stops everything instantly."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Speak the safewords aloud and physically practice the 3-tap signal together before every scene.
  headspace: Clear, reliable, automatic.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Signal Verification
    duration_min: 5
    description: Confirm verbal Red/Yellow/Green plus at least one non-verbal signal for tonight's gear/positions.
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Standard Traffic Light
    summary: Spoken Green / Yellow / Red for un-gagged, un-hooded play.
    adjustments:
      - Still pair with a 3-tap backup in case of breathlessness.
  heavier:
    title: Hooded / Gagged / Mitted Signal Set
    summary: Pair 3 rapid taps with a held bell/object drop and eye-blink/head-shake cue.
    adjustments:
      - Ensure hands or feet can always reach a surface to tap.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - If Yellow or Red was used, thank your partner immediately—calling a safeword is a sign of trust, never failure.
  physical:
    - Water and immediate release of any gear/restraints if Red was called.
  emotional:
    - Reaffirm safety and zero disappointment.
  next_day:
    - Check in next day if a Red stop occurred.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Were the signals easy to reach and hear in every position we used?
  check_in_window: Immediately post-scene.
