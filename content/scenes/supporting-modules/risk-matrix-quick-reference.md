risk_notes:
  - Breath play (choking/strangulation/airway restriction) carries permanent brain-injury and fatal risk even when "done carefully"—never improvise breath play.
  - Always keep EMT shears within arm's reach whenever rope, tape, or non-quick-release restraints are used.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: High-Risk Zones & Activities
      items:
        - id: rm-impact-zones
          label: Impact restricted to safe muscular zones (ass, upper back/shoulders, meaty thighs); kidneys/spine/neck/joints avoided
        - id: rm-breath
          label: Breath play / neck compression risks understood and excluded unless trained & explicitly negotiated
        - id: rm-restraint
          label: Nerve-compression checks (15-min finger/toe check) and EMT shears staged
        - id: rm-substances
          label: Alcohol / poppers / substance interactions reviewed (no heavy impact/bondage while impaired)
        - id: rm-marks
          label: Marks & visibility timeline verified against work/life calendar
  scripts:
    - context: Reviewing risk before a heavier scene
      line: "Shears are right here on the nightstand, kidneys and spine are off-limits, and we're checking hands every 15 minutes."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Scan this 5-point matrix whenever stepping up intensity or trying a new category.
  headspace: Calm, informed, zero ego about safety.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: 60-Second Safety Scan
    duration_min: 5
    description: Verify safe target zones, shears/keys staged, sobriety, and nerve-check cadence.
    check_in: true
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Pocket Card Review
    summary: Quick mental check of zones, sobriety, and safeword.
    adjustments:
      - Takes 30 seconds before any scene.
  heavier:
    title: Edge-Play Pre-Flight Audit
    summary: Full review of anatomy, biohazard/clean-up, emergency exit, and sober spotter plan.
    adjustments:
      - Required before any heavy restraint, suspension, or fluid/sharp edge play.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Inspect skin, circulation, and breathing immediately post-scene.
  physical:
    - Hydrate and rest.
  emotional:
    - Take pride in running tight, disciplined safety.
  next_day:
    - Check for delayed bruising or nerve tingling.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did all safety controls stay accessible throughout the scene?
  check_in_window: Immediately post-scene.
