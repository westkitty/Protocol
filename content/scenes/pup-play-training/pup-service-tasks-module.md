category: Pup Play & Training
category_slug: pup-play-training
subcategory: Service & Tasks
duration_min: 30
duration_max: 60
intensity: light
roles: ["Handler", "Pup"]
experience_level: beginner
tier: pro
materials:
  - Fetchable items (towel, slippers, water bottle, gear bag)
  - Knee pads or mat
prep_time_min: 5
risk_notes:
  - Do not ask a hooded or mitted pup to carry hot drinks, glass, or heavy/sharp objects.
  - Keep floors clear of tripping hazards during low-light fetches.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Task Scope
      items:
        - id: pst-safe-items
          label: Approved fetch/service items listed (unbreakable, safe to carry)
        - id: pst-mouth
          label: Mouth-carry vs. hand-carry rules agreed (hygiene & jaw comfort)
        - id: pst-posture
          label: Posture at handler's feet between tasks defined
  scripts:
    - context: Assigning a task
      line: "Go get my leather gloves from the bench and bring them straight back to my lap."
    - context: Rewarding completion
      line: "Right in my hand, no dropping. Good pup—take five seconds at my knee."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Stage the fetch items in known spots before starting.
    - Remove glass or hot liquids from the play area.
  headspace: Eager, useful, task-oriented.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Ready Posture
    duration_min: 5
    description: Pup settles at handler's feet, attentive for the first cue.
  - order: 2
    title: Sequential Fetch & Delivery
    duration_min: 20
    description: Handler issues 4–6 clear tasks (bring slippers, towel, brush, water bottle) with praise after each.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Foot / Boot Rest
    duration_min: 10
    description: Pup rests head on handler's knee or boot as reward.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Two-Task Lounge Helper
    summary: Simple fetch of remote/towel and resting by the couch.
    adjustments:
      - No strict speed or posture demands.
  heavier:
    title: Timed Service Circuit
    summary: Pup completes a 5-step gear staging circuit while hooded under a 10-minute timer.
    adjustments:
      - Only use shatterproof items and clear pathways.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Release from service posture and offer water.
  physical:
    - Check jaw if mouth-carrying was used; stretch knees/neck.
  emotional:
    - Verbal appreciation for effort and focus.
  next_day:
    - Note favorite tasks in Personal Library.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Which tasks felt most rewarding and immersive?
  check_in_window: End of session.
tags:
  - pup-play
  - service
  - tasks
  - fetch
---

Service doesn't have to mean heavy chores. This module gives pups tangible, satisfying ways to be useful and earn praise.
