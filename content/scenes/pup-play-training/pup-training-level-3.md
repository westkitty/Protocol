  - Public or semi-public spaces (leather bars, moshes, runs) introduce external strangers; establish strict third-party interaction rules beforehand.

negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Public & Third-Party Rules
      items:
        - id: p3-others
          label: Can other handlers/pups pet, play, or issue commands? (Yes / With Permission / Never)
        - id: p3-leash-off
          label: Off-leash radius and "check back in" interval agreed
        - id: p3-photos
          label: Photography/social media rules at the venue confirmed
    - section: Extended Endurance
      items:
        - id: p3-breaks
          label: Scheduled 15-minute hood-off hydration breaks set
        - id: p3-exit
          label: Code word or hand squeeze for immediate venue exit agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Setting third-party boundaries
      line: "Tonight you're mine—if someone asks to pet you, look to me first. If I nod, you can engage; if not, stay at my heel."
    - context: Discrete check-in in a crowded bar
      line: "Double squeeze on my calf means we step outside for air right now, no questions."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Confirm venue dress code, bag check, and quiet cool-down area before arriving.
    - Agree on transit plan home so neither partner is stranded if a scene ends early.
  headspace: Alert, grounded in dynamic despite external noise and distraction.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Pre-Entry Gear & Boundary Lock
    duration_min: 15
    description: Gear up privately or in designated space; confirm third-party and exit cues.
  - order: 2
    title: Heel Entry & Territory Survey
    duration_min: 20
    description: Enter space together; pup stays close while handler scouts exits, water, and seating.
  - order: 3
    title: Social / Mosh Block
    duration_min: 45
    description: Controlled interaction or mosh play within agreed permission rules.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Cool-Down & Hood-Off Break
    duration_min: 15
    description: Step to quiet corner or patio; remove hood, hydrate, and check status.
    check_in: true
  - order: 5
    title: Return & De-Gear
    duration_min: 25
    description: Transit home, full gear removal, shower, and grounded debrief.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Extended Home Marathon (No Public)
    summary: Run a 2-hour structured home session with distraction drills (music, doorbells) instead of a public venue.
    adjustments:
      - Practice staying in headspace while handler moves between rooms.
  heavier:
    title: Full Event / Leather Run Shift
    summary: Multi-hour event presence with formal handler check-ins every 45 minutes.
    adjustments:
      - Pre-pack electrolytes, spare dry shirt, and backup collar.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Full de-gear, cool shower, and electrolyte replenishment.
  physical:
    - Rest knees/back and inspect skin for heat rash or strap marks.
  emotional:
    - Debrief any unexpected third-party interactions or social overstimulation.
  next_day:
    - Midday check-in to process social/sub drop.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the third-party boundary rules hold up in the moment?
    - How was your stamina and heat management?
  check_in_window: Right after returning home and next day.

Level 3 takes pup play out of the living room into semi-public spaces—moshes, leather bars, or runs—where clear third-party rules and heat management are essential.
