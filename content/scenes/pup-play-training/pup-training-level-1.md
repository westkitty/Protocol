roles: ["Handler", "Pup"]

materials:
  - Collar (optional for Level 1)
  - Mat, rug, or knee pads
  - Water bowl or bottle
  - Small treats or praise cues
prep_time_min: 10
risk_notes:
  - Knee and wrist strain is common on hard floors; always use a mat or knee pads.
  - Keep verbal expectations simple so the pup can drop into non-verbal headspace without performance anxiety.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Headspace & Communication
      items:
        - id: p1-verbal
          label: Verbal vs. non-verbal expectations for the pup agreed
        - id: p1-commands
          label: Core 4 commands selected (Sit, Stay, Come, Down)
        - id: p1-rewards
          label: Reward style agreed (head scratches, verbal praise, treats)
    - section: Physical & Signals
      items:
        - id: p1-knees
          label: Knee pads / floor mat staged and joint comfort checked
        - id: p1-safeword
          label: Non-verbal stop signal (e.g., 3 rapid taps on thigh/floor) tested
  scripts:
    - context: Transitioning into pup headspace
      line: "Take three slow breaths. When I snap twice, you're off the clock—no human worries, just focus on my voice and your mat."
    - context: Reinforcing a command
      line: "Good boy. Hold that sit—stay steady until I release you."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Clear the floor of sharp objects and lay down a padded mat.
    - Test the non-verbal tap-out signal while still in human headspace.
  headspace: Playful, focused, low-pressure. Building trust in basic cues.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Drop-In & Breath Reset
    duration_min: 5
    description: Pup kneels on mat; handler uses steady voice and light shoulder/neck touch to cue headspace.
  - order: 2
    title: Core Command Drills
    duration_min: 15
    description: Practice Sit, Down, Stay, and Come with immediate tactile praise or treats.
  - order: 3
    title: Play & Fetch Interlude
    duration_min: 10
    description: Light tug or toy play to keep energy loose and fun.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Release & Grounding
    duration_min: 5
    description: Explicit release cue, water, and stretch out legs/wrists.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Seated / Couch-Side Headspace
    summary: Skip floor kneeling entirely; pup rests at handler's feet or beside them on the couch.
    adjustments:
      - Use hand signals and gentle collar/hair rubs only.
  heavier:
    title: Strict Silence & Hold
    summary: Pup remains non-verbal and holds each position for 60 seconds before praise.
    adjustments:
      - Add light verbal correction ("Ah-ah, try again") for broken stays.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Offer water in a glass or bowl and stretch hips, knees, and wrists.
  physical:
    - Light shoulder and lower-back rub to release floor posture tension.
  emotional:
    - Specific praise for what the pup nailed during drills.
  next_day:
    - Quick check-in on knee/wrist soreness.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Which command felt most natural, and did the pace let you drop into headspace?
  check_in_window: Immediately after release.
tags:

---

Level 1 focuses on building a reliable foundation: dropping human chatter, getting comfortable on the mat, and responding cleanly to four core handler cues.
