  - Kneeling for an entire weekend causes real joint injury; rotate between mat, couch, and human posture breaks.

  checklist:
    - section: Schedule & Sleep
      items:
        - id: pw-start-end
          label: Exact Friday start time and Sunday collar-off time locked
        - id: pw-sleep
          label: Sleeping arrangements (bed vs. floor pallet, gear off for sleep) confirmed
        - id: pw-meals
          label: Meal timing, human utensils vs. bowl, and dietary needs set
    - section: Activities & Free Time
      items:
        - id: pw-blocks
          label: Training, service, sexual, and quiet rest blocks mapped
        - id: pw-human-window
          label: Daily 20-minute out-of-role check-in window scheduled
  scripts:
    - context: Activating weekend protocol
      line: "Friday 7 PM to Sunday 4 PM, you're in pup protocol. Every morning at 9 AM we take 15 minutes out of role over coffee to check body and head."
    - context: Calling a posture break
      line: "Stretch break—up on the couch, human legs for ten minutes."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Prep groceries, clean mats, and charge phones/timers before Friday evening.
    - Agree on what happens if either partner needs to end the weekend early.
  headspace: Immersive, rhythmic, sustainable across 48 hours.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Friday Arrival & Collar Lock
    duration_min: 60
    description: Brief negotiation refresh, gear inspection, and formal start of weekend protocol.
  - order: 2
    title: Saturday Morning Routine & Drills
    duration_min: 180
    description: Morning check-in, feeding/water routine, and 30-minute command/fetch session.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Saturday Afternoon Service & Rest
    duration_min: 240
    description: Gear maintenance or light service tasks paired with quiet kennel/nap time.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Saturday Evening Scene
    duration_min: 180
    description: Main evening play block (leather, impact, or sexual service as negotiated).
    check_in: true
  - order: 5
    title: Sunday De-Escalation & Debrief
    duration_min: 120
    description: Gradual wind-down, collar removal ceremony, shared human meal, and full debrief.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 24-Hour Saturday-to-Sunday Pup Stay
    summary: Condense into Saturday noon to Sunday noon with relaxed lounging rules.
    adjustments:
      - Keep hood off except during dedicated 30-minute play blocks.
  heavier:
    title: Strict Non-Verbal Weekend
    summary: Pup stays non-verbal for the entire weekend outside the daily 15-minute human check-in.
    adjustments:
      - Use laminated cue cards or tap signals for urgent physical needs.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Formal collar removal, warm shower, and normal clothes.
  physical:
    - Stretch knees, hips, and shoulders; hydrate well.
  emotional:
    - Spend at least an hour together in equal, affectionate human space before parting.
  next_day:
    - Monday evening text check-in for post-weekend drop.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Which block felt most immersive, and where did energy dip?
    - Were the posture and rest breaks enough?
  check_in_window: Sunday afternoon and Monday evening.

A sustainable 24–48 hour pup weekend balances immersion with real-world physiology—knees, hydration, sleep, and structured human check-ins.
