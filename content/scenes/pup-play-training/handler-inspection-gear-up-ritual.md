title: Handler Inspection & Gear-Up Ritual
discreet_title: Equipment Check & Preparation Ritual
category: Pup Play & Training
category_slug: pup-play-training
subcategory: Rituals
duration_min: 30
duration_max: 60
intensity: medium
roles: ["Handler", "Pup"]
experience_level: beginner
tier: free
materials:
  - Collar and leash
  - Hood or ears (optional)
  - Tail, harness, and boots
  - Inspection surface, rug, or chair
  - Lube / cleaning wipes if relevant
prep_time_min: 10
risk_notes:
  - Check leather/neoprene edges and metal snaps for pinch points before tightening.
  - Keep collar snug (two-finger gap) — never restrict breathing or carotid blood flow.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Gear & Depth
      items:
        - id: hi-gear-list
          label: Exact gear items included and excluded confirmed
        - id: hi-depth
          label: Inspection depth agreed (visual only, clothed touch, or full skin/body check)
        - id: hi-commands
          label: Commands used during ritual confirmed (Present, Sit, Stay, Down)
    - section: Context & Headspace
      items:
        - id: hi-setting
          label: Private vs. semi-public setting confirmed
        - id: hi-headspace
          label: Headspace expectations (strict protocol vs. playful pup) aligned
  scripts:
    - context: Beginning the inspection
      line: "On your mat. Knees apart, hands behind your back, eyes down until I tell you to look up."
    - context: Examining gear
      line: "Leather's clean, buckles are straight. You took care of your gear—good pup."
    - context: Attaching the leash
      line: "This clip means you're on my lead for the next hour. Nod if you're ready."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Lay all gear out neatly on a towel or bench before calling the pup in.
    - Confirm safeword and non-verbal tap-out before buckling the collar or hood.
  headspace: Deliberate, ceremonial, focused on transition from everyday to dynamic.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Presentation
    duration_min: 5
    description: Pup assumes designated presentation posture. Handler walks a slow circle in silence.
  - order: 2
    title: Gear Check
    duration_min: 15
    description: Each piece is examined, named, and fitted. Handler comments on condition, posture, and fit.
  - order: 3
    title: Command Sequence
    duration_min: 10
    description: Basic commands (sit, stay, present, down) with immediate correction or praise.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Leash & Collar Lock
    duration_min: 10
    description: Final attachment of leash. Explicit acknowledgment of temporary or ongoing ownership for the scene.
  - order: 5
    title: Transition Cue
    duration_min: 5
    description: Clear signal that the inspection is complete and main play begins (or ends).
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 15-Minute Collar-Only Ritual
    summary: Focus solely on posture presentation, collar fitting, and three core commands.
    adjustments:
      - Skip boots, tail, and hood; keep it intimate and quick.
  heavier:
    title: Strict Full-Body & Gear Inspection
    summary: Includes detailed physical inspection, strict silence, and correction for any fidgeting.
    adjustments:
      - Add a 2-minute stillness hold between each gear piece.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Deliberate collar/hood removal ritual if temporary.
  physical:
    - Water, quiet time, and neck/shoulder rub.
  emotional:
    - Verbal check-in and option for continued contact or space.
  next_day:
    - Quick text check-in if the scene continued into deeper play.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the pacing of the gear-up help you drop into headspace?
    - Any gear fit adjustments needed for next time?
  check_in_window: Right after collar removal or end of full scene.
tags:

---

This ritual turns putting on gear from a rushed logistic step into the psychological doorway of the scene itself.
