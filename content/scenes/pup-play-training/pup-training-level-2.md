discreet_title: Equipment Integration & Structured Drills
category: Pup Play & Training
category_slug: pup-play-training
subcategory: Progressive Training
duration_min: 45
duration_max: 75
intensity: medium
roles: ["Handler", "Pup"]
experience_level: intermediate
tier: pro
materials:
  - Pup hood (neoprene or leather)
  - Collar and leash
  - Harness, tail, or mitts (optional)
  - Padded mat and water bowl
prep_time_min: 15
risk_notes:
  - Neoprene hoods trap heat and restrict peripheral vision/hearing; monitor overheating and hydration.
  - Never pull sharply on a collar attached to the neck; guide with steady tension or clip to a harness D-ring.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Gear & Comfort
      items:
        - id: p2-hood
          label: Hood fit, breathing vents, and heat tolerance confirmed
        - id: p2-mitts
          label: If using paw mitts, non-verbal tap-out signal adapted for closed hands
        - id: p2-leash
          label: Leash attachment point agreed (harness D-ring vs. collar)
    - section: Structure & Correction
      items:
        - id: p2-commands
          label: Intermediate cues added (Heel, Present, Bark/Speak, Kennel/Place)
        - id: p2-correction
          label: Correction style agreed (verbal reset vs. light tap vs. time-out)
  scripts:
    - context: Fitting the hood
      line: "Tell me if the muzzle feels tight or warm at any point—we can unbuckle the chin strap anytime without breaking the scene."
    - context: Leash handling
      line: "Feel the weight on the leash. Follow the gentle tension, no lunging."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Inspect all snaps, buckles, and hood vents before putting gear on.
    - Agree on a clear paw-tap or head-shake signal if mitts or hood limit speech.
  headspace: Focused, gear-anchored, attentive to tactile and leash cues.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Gear-Up & Fit Check
    duration_min: 10
    description: Handler fits collar, harness, and hood step-by-step, checking breathing and vision.
  - order: 2
    title: Leash & Heel Work
    duration_min: 15
    description: Practice walking/crawling on leash, stopping at handler's heel, and sitting on cue.
  - order: 3
    title: Hydration & Heat Check
    duration_min: 5
    description: Mandatory mid-scene water break; unfasten muzzle/hood if warm.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Kennel / Place & Present
    duration_min: 15
    description: Pup moves to designated spot on command, holds Present posture, and receives inspection/praise.
  - order: 5
    title: De-Gear Ritual
    duration_min: 10
    description: Hood and collar removed deliberately; handler wipes face and offers water.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Collar & Ears Only
    summary: Omit full hood and mitts to keep airflow and peripheral vision wide open.
    adjustments:
      - Focus on leash tension and heel/present posture.
  heavier:
    title: Full Gear + Mitts Obstacle Run
    summary: Pup navigates a short room course in full hood and mitts guided solely by leash and clicks.
    adjustments:
      - Shorten each drill to 5 minutes with frequent hydration checks.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Remove hood first to cool down, wipe sweat, and drink water.
  physical:
    - Check neck, chin, and wrists for strap chafing.
  emotional:
    - Acknowledge effort and discipline while transitioning back to human conversation.
  next_day:
    - Air out neoprene/leather gear and check in on energy levels.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - How did the hood affect your headspace and breathing?
    - Were leash cues clear without extra verbal instruction?
  check_in_window: Right after de-gearing and next morning.
tags:

---

Level 2 introduces full gear—hood, collar, and leash—and teaches both handler and pup how to communicate through tension, posture, and non-verbal cues.
