discreet_title: Contrast Feedback & Correction Scene
category: Pup Play & Training
category_slug: pup-play-training
subcategory: Dynamic Play
duration_min: 40
duration_max: 70
intensity: medium
roles: ["Handler", "Pup"]
experience_level: intermediate
tier: pro
materials:
  - Collar, leash, and toy/ball
  - Light paddle, crop, or rolled towel (if impact negotiated)
  - Corner mat or kennel spot
prep_time_min: 10
risk_notes:
  - Keep "bad pup" corrections clearly playful and bounded; never trigger real-world shame or harsh humiliation unless explicitly negotiated.
  - Always end the scene in "good pup" praise and warmth.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Mischief & Correction Rules
      items:
        - id: gpbp-triggers
          label: What counts as "mischief" agreed (stealing sock, barking out of turn, nudging hand)
        - id: gpbp-correction
          label: Approved correction tools/methods (time-out corner, stern voice, light swat)
        - id: gpbp-impact
          label: Impact zones and max intensity for corrections confirmed
    - section: Praise & Repair
      items:
        - id: gpbp-repair
          label: Exact behavior that earns "good pup" status back (sit still, apologize gesture, fetch)
  scripts:
    - context: Calling out mischief
      line: "You know you're not supposed to chew on that. Corner. Nose down until I call you."
    - context: Restoring Good Pup status
      line: "That's it—steady, quiet, looking right at me. Good boy. Come get your praise."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Agree beforehand that "bad pup" is a theatrical game, not genuine anger.
    - Test any impact correction at 20% intensity first.
  headspace: Mischievous, responsive, clear contrast between sternness and affection.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Good Pup Baseline
    duration_min: 10
    description: Establish calm obedience, praise, and physical affection.
  - order: 2
    title: Playful Mischief & Correction
    duration_min: 15
    description: Pup tests a boundary; handler delivers firm, negotiated correction and brief time-out.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Repair & Heavy Praise
    duration_min: 15
    description: Pup earns back approval through a clean command sequence and receives intense praise/reward.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Verbal & Time-Out Only
    summary: No physical impact; corrections use stern tone, leash tug, and 2-minute corner sit.
    adjustments:
      - Focus on contrast between stern voice and warm head scratches.
  heavier:
    title: Bratty Pup + Structured Spanking
    summary: Longer mischief escalation with negotiated over-the-knee hand spanking or leather strap before repair.
    adjustments:
      - Confirm safeword check mid-correction.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - End firmly in "good pup" affection before removing gear.
  physical:
    - Rub any reddened spots and offer water.
  emotional:
    - Reaffirm out of role that the pup did great and no real frustration existed.
  next_day:
    - Quick check-in to ensure no lingering emotional sting.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the balance between correction and praise feel hot and safe?
  check_in_window: Immediately post-scene.
tags:

---

Contrast makes dynamics electric. This scene uses playful mischief, bounded correction, and generous repair praise to create a tight emotional arc.
