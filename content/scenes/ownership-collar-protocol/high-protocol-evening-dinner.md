materials:
  - Formal or leather-adjacent attire (harness under shirt, polished boots, day collar)
  - Pre-planned menu & table setting
  - Serving tray, cloth napkin, water/wine glasses
prep_time_min: 45
risk_notes:
  - High protocol should never prevent the server from eating properly or using the restroom; build explicit "eat/rest" cues into the evening.
  - Keep hot dishes and sharp knives handled safely—no blindfolds or restrictive wrist cuffs while carrying hot food.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Protocol Rules
      items:
        - id: hpd-address
          label: Forms of address and eye-contact rules (eyes down vs. permission to look) set
        - id: hpd-speech
          label: Speech rules (speak only when addressed, phrasing) agreed
        - id: hpd-posture
          label: Standing/kneeling/seating posture while Sir eats confirmed
    - section: Logistics & Comfort
      items:
        - id: hpd-eat
          label: When and where the boy eats his own dinner scheduled
        - id: hpd-alcohol
          label: Alcohol limits (max 1–2 drinks if playing afterward) agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Beginning service
      line: "Evening starts when the front door locks. Pour water first, stand to my left until I tell you to sit."
    - context: Granting permission to eat
      line: "Good service. Fix your plate and sit beside me—keep your collar on, but eat comfortably."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Prep as much cooking in advance as possible so kitchen timing doesn't break protocol.
    - Stage knee cushion beside Sir's chair if kneeling at the table is included.
  headspace: Crisp, attentive, refined, Old Guard leather formality.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Arrival & Coat/Drink Service
    duration_min: 20
    description: Boy greets Sir (and any guests) at the door, takes coats, and serves first drinks cleanly.
  - order: 2
    title: Dinner Service & Table Protocol
    duration_min: 60
    description: Courses served in silence or formal address; boy stands at attention or kneels when not plating.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Boy's Meal & Quiet Reset
    duration_min: 30
    description: Sir releases boy to eat a full meal calmly; brief posture check.
  - order: 4
    title: After-Dinner Cigar/Drink & Scene Transition
    duration_min: 45
    description: Coffee/drink service followed by boots-off or transition into evening bedroom play.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Two-Course Dress-Up Dinner
    summary: Keep protocol only during appetizer and main course, then relax into normal conversation.
    adjustments:
      - Sit together at the table after plating; keep titles and posture cues light.
  heavier:
    title: Hosted Leather Dinner Party
    summary: 4–6 invited leather friends where the boy serves the entire table under formal protocol.
    adjustments:
      - Brief guests in advance on how to address the boy (or to route requests through Sir).
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Clear only essential dishes, leave heavy cleanup for morning, and transition to rest.
  physical:
    - Take off tight dress boots/harness and hydrate.
  emotional:
    - Praise specific moments of poise and attention.
  next_day:
    - Share breakfast together out of protocol.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the protocol feel sharp and sexy rather than stressful?
    - Was the kitchen/service pacing realistic?
  check_in_window: Next morning over coffee.

High protocol is about precision, presence, and atmosphere—turning an ordinary dinner into an unforgettable display of discipline and care.
