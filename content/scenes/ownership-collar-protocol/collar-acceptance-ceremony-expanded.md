# Collar Acceptance Ceremony (Expanded)

A collar ceremony marks a clear transition. The words and actions make the container explicit so both people know what has been agreed and what has not. This version supplies full sample scripts plus modular dialogue options and the physical ways to invoke each section.

## Full Sample Script (Adaptable)

**Owner:**  
"We are here to mark an agreement. This collar means [specific meaning you both chose: temporary ownership for the weekend / ongoing protocol with monthly review / scene-only]. It does not override your limits or your right to stop. Do you understand?"

**Collared:**  
"Yes."

**Owner:**  
"State what you are agreeing to."

**Collared:**  
"I agree to wear this collar under the terms we discussed. I will follow the protocols we set. I can use my safeword or ask to pause at any time."

**Owner:**  
"I agree to respect every limit, provide the structure we outlined, and remove or pause this collar if you ask or if it is no longer right. Present."

*(Collar is fastened.)*

**Owner:**  
"The collar is on. The agreement is active."

## Dialogue Options & Invocation Methods

### Opening the Ceremony
**Options:**
- "Kneel. We begin."
- "Stand in front of me. Look at the collar."
- "This is the start of the agreement."

**How to invoke:**
- Place the collar on a cloth between you and wait until they are in position.
- Dim the lights, then speak the opening line.
- Hold the collar in both hands at chest height as the visual cue to begin.

### Statement of Meaning
**Options:**
- "Tell me what this collar means to you."
- "Repeat the terms we agreed."
- Owner states the meaning, then asks "Is that accurate?"

**How to invoke:**
- Hand them a short written card with the key points to read aloud.
- Remain silent after asking until they finish speaking.
- Place a hand on their shoulder only after they have spoken.

### Fastening
**Options:**
- "Chin up. Hold still."
- "Breathe. I'm putting it on now."
- "Nod if the fit is correct."

**How to invoke:**
- Stand behind them, show the open collar in front of their face first, then bring it around.
- Fasten it, then gently tug the ring once and ask for confirmation.
- For lockable collars: show the key, lock it, and place the key where you both agreed.

### Closing Acknowledgment
**Options:**
- "The collar is on. You are under this agreement until [end condition]."
- "Rise. The ceremony is complete."
- "Look at me. We are clear."

**How to invoke:**
- Step back, make eye contact, and speak the closing line.
- Offer a hand to help them rise.
- Clip a leash briefly as a final physical marker, then unclip or leave it according to prior agreement.

## Why This Works
Explicit language prevents mismatched expectations. The physical act of fastening becomes meaningful because the words have already defined it. Both people hear the limits and the exit conditions out loud.

## Modular Additions
- Include a short written document both sign or initial.
- Add a specific protocol that begins immediately (e.g., title use, kneeling on entry).
- Temporary vs. review-date versions ("We will revisit this on [date]").
