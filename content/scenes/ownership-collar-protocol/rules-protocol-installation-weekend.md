subcategory: Protocol Installation
duration_min: 1440
duration_max: 2880
intensity: progressive
roles: ["Sir / Master", "Boy / Submissive"]
experience_level: intermediate
tier: pro
materials:
  - Printed 5-to-7 rule sheet
  - Collar or wrist band
  - Timer / phone reminders
prep_time_min: 20
risk_notes:
  - Installing too many rules at once causes cognitive overload and failure; cap at 5–7 clear, testable rules.
  - Distinguish between "practice corrections" (light, instructional) and "disciplinary scenes" (pre-negotiated).
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Rule Selection
      items:
        - id: rpi-count
          label: Maximum 5–7 concrete, observable rules selected (no vague "be good" rules)
        - id: rpi-examples
          label: Morning check-in text, posture cue, permission-to-speak, hydration, and bedtime ritual defined
        - id: rpi-correction
          label: Standard correction for a missed rule agreed (e.g., verbal reset + 2-minute kneel)
    - section: Sustainability
      items:
        - id: rpi-carryover
          label: Which 2–3 rules will remain active on Monday morning agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Installing a rule
      line: "Rule three: whenever I enter the room, you pause what you're doing and give me eye contact until I wave you back."
    - context: Coaching a miss
      line: "You forgot to ask before sitting. Stand up, reset, and try it again cleanly."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Write the 5–7 rules on a single index card and review them together before Friday night.
  headspace: Structured, instructional, steady.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Friday Rule Briefing & Activation
    duration_min: 45
    description: Read each rule aloud, demonstrate posture/responses, and lock in the weekend.
  - order: 2
    title: Saturday Morning & Midday Practice
    duration_min: 240
    description: Live practice of rules during ordinary errands, coffee, and meals with gentle coaching.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Saturday Evening Protocol Test
    duration_min: 120
    description: Higher-strictness evening block testing all rules together, rewarded with praise or play.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Sunday Audit & Monday Carryover
    duration_min: 90
    description: Review which rules felt natural vs. clunky and pick 2–3 to keep long-term.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 3-Rule Saturday Trial
    summary: Pick just three simple rules (greeting, water, bedtime text) for a single Saturday.
    adjustments:
      - Zero physical corrections; verbal coaching only.
  heavier:
    title: Strict Inspection & Merit/Demerit Ledger
    summary: Track rule adherence through the weekend with an evening accountability review.
    adjustments:
      - Pre-negotiate any physical accountability (e.g., 10 strap strokes max) beforehand.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Step out of strict protocol Sunday afternoon for a relaxed walk or meal.
  physical:
    - Rest and hydrate.
  emotional:
    - Focus feedback on progress and effort, not perfection.
  next_day:
    - Check in Monday evening on how the carryover rules fit into the workday.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Which rule felt hottest and easiest to keep?
    - Which rule felt artificial and should be dropped?
  check_in_window: Sunday afternoon and Monday night.
tags:

Rules only stick when they are practiced in real life. Use a weekend to install, test, and refine 5–7 concrete habits before taking them into the workweek.
