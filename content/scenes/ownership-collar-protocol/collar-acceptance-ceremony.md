risk_notes:
  - A collar ceremony carries real emotional weight; never rush into ongoing collaring before negotiating exit terms and expectations.
  - Ensure any locking collar has a quick-release key or hex tool accessible at all times for medical/safety emergencies.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Meaning & Scope
      items:
        - id: col-type
          label: Collar type clarified (Play/Scene Collar, Consideration Collar, or Full Ownership Collar)
        - id: col-wear
          label: When and where the collar is worn (24/7, home only, scenes only) agreed
        - id: col-expectations
          label: 3–5 concrete commitments from each partner stated plainly
    - section: Safety & Exit
      items:
        - id: col-emergency
          label: Spare key / Allen wrench location shared with both partners
        - id: col-return
          label: How the collar can be paused or returned respectfully if needed
  scripts:
    - context: Offering the collar
      line: "This collar represents my commitment to lead, protect, and hold you accountable—and your choice to place your submission in my hands."
    - context: Accepting the collar
      line: "I accept this collar freely, knowing my limits are respected and my voice remains mine."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Clean and condition the collar beforehand; lay it on a tray or cloth.
    - Agree on whether the ceremony is private or witnessed by close leather/kink family.
  headspace: Solemn, grounded, deeply intentional.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Stillness & Presentation
    duration_min: 5
    description: Boy kneels or stands at attention; Sir approaches with the collar on a tray.
  - order: 2
    title: Statement of Commitments
    duration_min: 10
    description: Both partners speak their agreed commitments clearly and without rushing.
  - order: 3
    title: Fitting & Locking
    duration_min: 10
    description: Collar is fastened; two-finger fit checked; brief silent eye contact or kiss.
    check_in: true
  - order: 4
    title: Sealing & Celebration
    duration_min: 15
    description: Transition into quiet embrace, toast, or negotiated celebratory scene.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Consideration / Trial Collar

    adjustments:
      - Use a buckled leather or discreet chain day-collar.
  heavier:
    title: Formal Leather-Family Witnessed Ceremony
    summary: Structured ceremony with 2–4 trusted community witnesses and formal charge.
    adjustments:
      - Pre-brief guests on silence during vows and confidentiality.

  immediate:
    - Hold each other quietly to let the emotional intensity settle.
  physical:
    - Check collar comfort and skin clearance around the neck.
  emotional:
    - Celebrate the milestone out of protocol afterward.
  next_day:
    - Check in on how wearing the collar feels in daily life.

A collar is only as strong as the clarity behind it. This ceremony grounds the symbol in explicit, mutual commitments and real-world safety.
