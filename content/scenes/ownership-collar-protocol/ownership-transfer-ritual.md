materials:
  - Collar or symbolic token (leather cuff, dog tag)
  - Written limits & protocol summary card
prep_time_min: 15
risk_notes:
  - The submissive's active, enthusiastic consent is the hinge of any transfer—never treat a partner as property without their direct voice.
  - Incoming Dominant must receive full health, safeword, and hard-limit briefing beforehand.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Transfer Parameters
      items:
        - id: otr-duration
          label: Temporary (exact hours/weekend) vs. permanent/ongoing transition specified
        - id: otr-rules
          label: Which existing rules carry over and which reset agreed
        - id: otr-contact
          label: Contact rules with Outgoing Dominant during temporary transfer set
    - section: Safety Handoff
      items:
        - id: otr-brief
          label: Health, triggers, safewords, and aftercare preferences shared cleanly
  scripts:
    - context: Outgoing Dominant releasing
      line: "I release my hold on you for tonight and place your care and direction in his hands, with your full consent."
    - context: Incoming Dominant receiving
      line: "I accept responsibility for your safety, your limits, and your service while you're under my hand."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Hold a plain-clothes three-way alignment talk first.
    - Confirm the exact return cue if the transfer is temporary.
  headspace: Respectful, ceremonial, clear chain of accountability.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Three-Way Alignment Confirmation
    duration_min: 10
    description: Submissive verbally confirms readiness to both Dominants.
  - order: 2
    title: Symbolic Release & Acceptance
    duration_min: 10
    description: Outgoing Dominant unclips leash or removes temporary band; Incoming Dominant fastens their cuff or tag.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: First Command Under New Authority
    duration_min: 10
    description: Incoming Dominant gives a clear, grounding directive to anchor the new dynamic.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: Evening Scene Loan
    summary: Simple 3-hour handoff for a single evening scene at a party or home.
    adjustments:
      - Use a wrist cuff or dog tag instead of changing a primary collar.
  heavier:
    title: Formal Dynamic Transition
    summary: Structured closure of a past dynamic and formal opening of a new one.
    adjustments:
      - Separate the closure ritual and new collar acceptance by at least 24 hours if emotions run high.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Check in with the submissive privately after the ritual.
  physical:
    - Water and grounding touch.
  emotional:
    - Ensure no lingering guilt or awkwardness between any of the three parties.
  next_day:
    - Follow-up text from Incoming (and Outgoing if ongoing) Dominant.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did all three people feel respected and clear on boundaries?
  check_in_window: Immediately post-ritual and next day.

Whether lending a boy for an evening or formally transitioning dynamics, this ritual keeps agency, dignity, and safety front and center.
