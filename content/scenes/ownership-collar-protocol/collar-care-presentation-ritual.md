duration_max: 45

roles: ["Sir / Handler", "Boy / Pup"]
experience_level: beginner
tier: pro
materials:
  - Collar (leather or metal)
  - Microfiber cloth, leather balm, or metal polish
  - Presentation tray or towel
prep_time_min: 5
risk_notes:
  - Never apply harsh chemical polishes to leather or skin-contact hardware without wiping and buffing dry thoroughly.
  - Allow leather balm to absorb and buff completely so it does not stain neck skin or shirts.
negotiation:
  checklist:
    - section: Ritual Structure
      items:
        - id: ccp-who
          label: Who cleans the collar (Boy cleans it under Sir's watch, or Sir cleans it while Boy kneels)
        - id: ccp-speech
          label: Silent contemplation vs. spoken reflection during care
        - id: ccp-recollar
          label: Formal re-fastening cue agreed
  scripts:
    - context: Handing over the collar for care
      line: "Take it off, lay it on the cloth, and condition every inch of the leather. Take your time."
    - context: Re-fastening
      line: "Clean, supple, ready. Chin up—let's put it back where it belongs."
pre_scene:
  steps:
    - Stage conditioner, soft cloths, and a clean surface.
  headspace: Quiet, meditative, grounding.
beats:
  - order: 1
    title: Removal & Inspection
    duration_min: 5
    description: Collar is removed gently and inspected for wear, salt, or loose hardware.
  - order: 2
    title: Cleaning & Conditioning
    duration_min: 15
    description: Slow, deliberate wiping, conditioning, and buffing of leather and D-ring.
    check_in: true
  - order: 3
    title: Presentation & Re-Collaring
    duration_min: 10
    description: Boy presents the buffed collar on both palms; Sir inspects and fastens it back on.
variations:
  lighter:
    title: 10-Minute Sunday Wipe-Down
    summary: Quick cloth wipe and check-in while sitting together on the couch.
    adjustments:
      - Pair with a simple weekly check-in question.
  heavier:
    title: Full Gear Altar Maintenance
    summary: Condition collar, cuffs, harness, and flogger in one silent 45-minute ritual.
    adjustments:
      - Boy remains shirtless in kneeling posture throughout.
aftercare:
  immediate:
    - Wash hands of balm/polish before touching face or eyes.
  physical:
    - Neck rub after re-fastening.
  emotional:
    - Quiet appreciation for care and consistency.
  next_day:
    - None required unless paired with a heavier scene.
debrief:
  prompts:
    - Did the ritual help reconnect you both to what the collar means?
  check_in_window: Immediately after re-collaring.
tags:

Maintenance is devotion made visible. This quiet ritual turns leather care into a grounding reset for any collared dynamic.
