# Inspection & Service Language Pack

A modular collection of phrases and the physical or contextual ways to invoke them. Use these as building blocks inside any inspection, gear-up, or service scene.

## Inspection Phrases

### Posture
- "Present." → Invoke by pointing to the mat or snapping once.
- "Eyes down." → Invoke by placing a hand lightly on the back of the head or simply waiting in silence.
- "Still." → Invoke by holding up a closed fist.

### Gear
- "Show me the collar." → Invoke by holding out an open hand.
- "Fix the strap." → Invoke by tapping the specific buckle.
- "Good condition." → Invoke by a single nod after examining the piece.

### Transition
- "Inspection complete." → Invoke by stepping back two paces.
- "You're on my lead." → Invoke by clipping the leash and giving one gentle tug.

## Service Phrases

### Starting Work
- "Begin." → Invoke by placing the object (boot, cloth, gear) in front of them.
- "Take your time." → Invoke by sitting back and remaining quiet for the first several minutes.

### During Work
- "Slower." → Invoke by resting a hand on their shoulder.
- "Show me." → Invoke by extending an open palm.
- "That's better." → Invoke after they correct a mistake, with a brief touch or nod.

### Ending Service
- "Present the result." → Invoke by pointing to a clear space on the floor or table.
- "You may rise." → Invoke by offering a hand or pointing upward.

## Usage Notes
Pair any phrase with a consistent physical cue so the sub learns the signal even if the words change. Silence after a command is often more powerful than additional explanation.
