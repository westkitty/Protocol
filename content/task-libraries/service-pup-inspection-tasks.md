# Service, Pup & Inspection Task Library

Reusable, specific tasks that can be dropped into any service, pup, or inspection scene. Each entry includes a short description and a common way to invoke it.

## Leather & Gear Tasks
1. Wipe down boots with a damp cloth — Invoke by placing the boots and cloth on the floor.
2. Apply conditioner to a jacket — Invoke by handing over the jacket and the tin.
3. Check all buckles for straightness — Invoke by saying "Buckles" and pointing.
4. Present the collar for inspection — Invoke by holding out an open hand.
5. Polish one boot to a set standard — Invoke by setting a timer and the boot in front of them.
6. Store gear in its proper place — Invoke by pointing to the storage area after use.
7. Report any damage or wear found — Invoke by requiring a short verbal or written note at the end.
8. Warm the leather with hands before dressing — Invoke by handing the piece over and waiting.

## Pup-Specific Tasks
9. Hold a "present" position for 60 seconds — Invoke with the command and a visible timer.
10. Fetch a named piece of gear — Invoke by naming it and pointing to where it should be brought.
11. Walk at heel for one loop of the room — Invoke by clipping the leash and starting to walk.
12. Practice three basic commands in sequence — Invoke by saying "Commands" and listing them.
13. Rest head on handler's boot for two minutes — Invoke by tapping the boot.
14. Carry a small item in the mouth across the room — Invoke by placing the item and pointing to the destination.
15. Wait in a down-stay while the handler leaves the room briefly — Invoke by the command and stepping out.

## Service & Domestic Tasks
16. Pour a drink and present it — Invoke by setting the glass down and waiting.
17. Clear plates between courses — Invoke by a nod when finished eating.
18. Kneel beside the chair for a set time — Invoke by pointing to the cushion.
19. Prepare the kneeling mat and supplies — Invoke by naming the task before the scene starts.
20. Give a short status report on request — Invoke by saying "Report."
21. Adjust room lighting or music on command — Invoke with a single word ("Lights," "Music").
22. Fold and store clothing items — Invoke by placing the items in a pile.
23. Run a bath or shower to a set temperature — Invoke by stating the task and the time it should be ready.

## Inspection Tasks
24. Stand for a slow visual circle — Invoke by saying "Present" and walking around.
25. Show hands front and back — Invoke by holding out your own hands as the model.
26. Open the mouth for inspection — Invoke by a light tap under the chin.
27. Display gear already worn — Invoke by saying "Show me" and naming the piece.
28. Hold still while straps are adjusted — Invoke by placing hands on the strap and saying "Still."
29. Answer a fit question with only "yes" or "no" — Invoke by asking the question and waiting.
30. Demonstrate a required posture from memory — Invoke by naming the posture.

## Protocol & Position Tasks
31. Kneel when the owner sits — Invoke by the owner sitting without further words.
32. Use the agreed title in every sentence for ten minutes — Invoke by stating the rule and starting a timer.
33. Ask permission before leaving the room — Invoke by establishing the rule at the start of the evening.
34. Wait to eat until given permission — Invoke by serving the food and remaining silent.
35. Maintain eye contact or eyes-down as ordered — Invoke by a single gesture (finger to eyes or downward).

## Additional Modular Tasks (36–55)
36. Write a short end-of-day note.
37. Set out aftercare items in advance.
38. Time a stillness hold and report the duration.
39. Practice a specific phrase until it is smooth.
40. Inspect and report on the condition of one gear item.
41. Serve water at set intervals.
42. Maintain a chosen posture during a conversation.
43. Retrieve a dropped item without being asked.
44. Signal readiness with a pre-agreed gesture only.
45. Count aloud during a hold.
46. Present both wrists for cuff or rope placement.
47. Describe what they are feeling in one sentence when asked.
48. Reset a position after a correction without comment.
49. Carry a tray or object at a set height.
50. End a task by returning to a neutral kneel and waiting.
51. Confirm understanding of a new rule by repeating it.
52. Choose and prepare one service task from a short list.
53. Hold a leash in the mouth while waiting.
54. Perform a gear check on themselves and report.
55. Close the scene by returning all items to their places.

## How to Use This Library
Pick 3–7 tasks for any given scene. State them at the start or reveal them one at a time. Pair each task with a consistent invocation cue so the action becomes automatic. After the scene, note which tasks created the strongest focus or drop.
